Put this on your iPhone

The Home Screen shortcut has to be created in Safari. A downloaded file is a backup you can AirDrop.

  1. Open this site in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

After that, Names opens like any other app and keeps working offline.
